Fix the verdict-gate correctness issues surfaced by the Phase 04.1.1 batch code review (2 specialized agents over the combined Waves 1+2+2b estimator diff), BEFORE the Plan 04.1.1-03 production-rep records a four-criterion verdict on the corrected estimator.

**THE BLOCKER (Model QA, confirmed):** `parametric_bootstrap_lr` has a time-origin asymmetry. The observed leg computes `_hawkes_loglik_vectorized(..., leg_0_times, leg_1_times)` on ABSOLUTE epoch timestamps (~1.7e9), and the null replicates fit the LS estimator on t0-rescaled arrays (`_fit_hawkes_ls_for_null` does `s0 = sim_0 - t0`) but then SCORE on absolute `sim_*_abs` arrays. The Hawkes LL integral term is `baseline·t_end`; scoring params fit on a `[0, Δ]` window against `t_end ≈ 1.7e9` inflates the LL (this is the signature behind the spurious 6.05M observed_stat). The inflation does not provably cancel in the LR difference (NHPP and Hawkes baselines differ), so the bootstrap p-value can be biased in an undetermined direction — corrupting the LR criterion of the verdict gate.

**NEEDS WORK #1 (Code Reviewer):** the LR observed Hawkes fit re-runs its own full-panel AIC grid (`fit_hawkes_expkern(leg_0_times, leg_1_times)`), selecting a β independently of the orchestrator's train-fit β. So the gate's η-floor / CI checks (from the train fit) and the LR leg (from a separate full-panel fit) may use DIFFERENT β — the four criteria are not provably at one coherent (β, η).

**NEEDS WORK #2 (Model QA):** (a) `_fit_with_scipy_canonical_ll` has bounds `(1e-12, None)` with NO stationarity constraint — an explosive ρ(α/β) >= 1 fit could pass the η-floor gate as an artifact (did not bite on the real panel at η≈0.63, but must be closed); (b) the profile-likelihood `ETA_GRID_DEFAULT` starts at 0.02, so `branching_ci_excludes_zero` (lower > 0) could be a grid-floor artifact rather than a real LL crossing.

These are IMPLEMENTATION-CORRECTNESS fixes that enforce already-locked conditions (scale-consistent canonical LL; the pre-registered stationarity condition `||α/β||_∞ < 1` in §Kernel Forms). NO verdict threshold or criterion changes.
