## Decisions Made

1. **η-coherence band sourced from the LS-fallback fit_report.json itself.** The `branching_ratio_ci` profile-likelihood path in `ae9e3ba17900/fit_report.json` is the INDEPENDENT estimator that disagreed with the broken LS-fallback by ~1000×. Using its CI as the acceptance band is methodologically clean because (a) the profile-likelihood path is the trusted estimator on this substrate, (b) the band is read at executor time, not invented during plan authoring, (c) the LS-fallback's degenerate `branching_ratio = 3.07e-4` is provably outside the band — so the band correctly rejects the broken estimator.

2. **DECAY_GRID extension is a NEW lock, not a revision of any existing threshold.** This is explicit in the AF-12 OUT-OF-SCOPE list: "NO change to η-floor 0.2 / LR α 0.01 / KS α 0.05 / Q-9 floor 300 — the η-coherence band is a NEW lock, not a revision." The original Phase 3 thresholds carry forward unchanged; 04.1.1 layers acceptance criteria ON TOP of them.

3. **scipy L-BFGS-B over Nelder-Mead or BFGS.** L-BFGS-B is the natural choice for positivity-bounded smooth optimization of a vectorized log-likelihood with 6 parameters. Nelder-Mead would be derivative-free but slower and less reliable on smooth LL surfaces; BFGS lacks positivity bounds. The pre-registered choice forecloses on optimizer-shopping post-failure.

4. **BLOCKER policy instead of a Fallback C, D, E ...** Pre-registering a third (or fourth) fallback would invite method-shopping (try every optimizer until one converges to something publishable). The BLOCKER policy enforces honest investigation when both pre-registered paths fail — surfacing the failure to a human reviewer rather than to a silently-broken estimator.

5. **Atomic single-task commit.** The plan body is explicit: "DO NOT touch any other file in this plan." The pre-existing `.planning/ROADMAP.md` modification (Phase 04.1.1 entry inserted by `/gsd:plan-phase 04.1.1`) is left for the final summary commit per `<state_updates>` workflow convention, NOT folded into the AF-03 commit. This preserves the AF-03 commit's audit purity (single-file docs commit; no spillover into source or other planning artifacts).
