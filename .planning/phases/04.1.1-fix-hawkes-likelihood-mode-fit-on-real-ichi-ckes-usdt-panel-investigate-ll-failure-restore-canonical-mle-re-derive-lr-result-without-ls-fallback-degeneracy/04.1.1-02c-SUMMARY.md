## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Flat 1e18 stationarity plateau regressed the synthetic η=0.5 recovery**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (post-GREEN full-suite regression of `test_hawkes_fit.py`)
- **Issue:** The plan specified `if ρ(α/β)>=1: return 1e18` inside `neg_ll`. A flat 1e18 has zero gradient and is unscaled vs the ~O(10³) LL. At the synthetic fixture's true β=0.001 the fixed multi-start α (0.01/0.1/0.3) is explosive (ρ(α/β)=2c/β ≫ 1), so all starts landed on the flat plateau, L-BFGS-B could not descend, the β=0.001 fit RAISED (all multi-starts failed), and AIC fell to a wrong β with η≈0.026/0.16. `test_likelihood_mode_eta_recovers_synthetic_ground_truth` and `test_likelihood_mode_eta_within_v2_acceptance[synthetic]` failed (η=0.0256 not in [0.40,0.60]).
- **Fix:** (a) scale the multi-start initial α to a fixed *initial branching ratio* (0.1/0.3/0.5) via `c = η_init·β/2`, so every start is stationary at every DECAY_GRID β; (b) in `neg_ll`, when ρ≥1 evaluate the LL at α clipped to ρ=0.999 and add a smooth quadratic barrier in the violation (well-scaled, gradient-bearing) instead of a flat 1e18 plateau. `_reject_nonstationary` itself still returns ≥1e18 (test/external contract unchanged).
- **Files modified:** `analysis/src/abrigo_x402/dgp/hawkes_fit.py`
- **Verification:** AIC now selects β=0.001 η=0.4887 (matches the fixture true η=0.5; matches the pre-guard genuine optimum confirmed by a direct unconstrained probe); all 6 DECAY_GRID fits succeed and are stationary (η ∈ {0.63, 0.49, 0.16, 0.026, 0.0017, 0.0}, all <1). `tests/test_hawkes_fit.py` 10 passed/256s; `tests/test_lr_test.py` 10 passed/445s.
- **Committed in:** `dc489de`
