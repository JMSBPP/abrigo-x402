1. Reads the four sidecars from `data/raw/ichi/<pool>/`:
   - `pool_events.jsonl` (832 rows; the one carrying `timeStamp` hex field)
   - `tx_logs.jsonl` (optional; Transfer-companion sidecar)

   - `fx_snap.jsonl`

4. Writes `data/raw/ichi/<pool>/<from_block>_<to_block>.parquet` via `write_panel` with the 6 PANEL-02 header fields.
